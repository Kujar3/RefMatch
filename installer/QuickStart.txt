REFMATCH - QUICK START
======================

RefMatch is installed. In FL Studio:

1. Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (or "Start scan").
2. Open the Mixer, select the MASTER track and add RefMatch in the LAST
   effect slot (below your limiter, if you use one). Pick the VST3 version.
3. Keep FL's Master fader at 0 dB - RefMatch listens before that fader.

COMPARE WITH A REFERENCE
- Drag a released song you love (same genre) onto the strip at the top of
  RefMatch, or click "Load". WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF or OGG.
- Drag across the waveform to loop the best part (e.g. the drop), then play
  the same part of your own song and click the round "reset" arrow.
- Click MIX / REFERENCE while FL plays. "Level match" plays the reference at
  your mix's loudness, so you judge the sound - not which one is louder.

READ THE RESULTS
- Tonal balance: mint line = your mix, purple area = the reference. The bars
  show which ranges you have more (up) or less (down) of.
- Loudness & dynamics: how loud, how peaky and how dynamic your mix is.
- Stereo image: how wide each range is. Bass should stay in the middle.
- What to try: the biggest differences first, each with a concrete fix.
  Make small moves, listen again, press reset after changing your mix.

When you export, RefMatch always passes your song through untouched.
The "?" button in the top-right corner of the plugin explains everything.
